// 04 ZERO-CLASH AUDIT PROCEDURE

Three-Tier Quality Control Protocol

Every sheet of reinforcement drawings and every line of BBS undergoes strict peer verification before client submittal.

Level 01 Check

Detailer Check

The lead detailer verifies rebar modeling geometry against the structural design documents, checking covers, lap lengths, and bend radius allowances.

  • Verify concrete clear covers against architectural finish specs
  • Confirm bar diameters and spacing against Engineer of Record (EOR) plans
  • Validate standard hook dimensions and pin diameters per standard code
Level 02 Check

Technical Review

A Senior Structural Rebar Specialist audits joint congestion, constructability, handling weights, and mechanical splice coupler layouts.

  • Beam-column joint congestion simulation & vibrator clearance
  • Cross-check with MEP penetrations, core drilling zones, and embeds
  • Optimization check for stock lengths (12m/14m/18m or 40ft/60ft)
Level 03 Check

Final QA/QC

Independent Quality Assurance Manager completes a cross-referencing audit between the 2D placement drawings, 3D model, and BBS cut-lists.

  • 100% mathematical reconciliation of BBS bar quantities vs drawing marks
  • Sign-off stamped for direct fabrication & site release
  • Transmittal package prepared with revision changelogs and RFI history

// 09 TECHNICAL INQUIRIES

Frequently Answered Technical Questions

Essential clarifications on deliverables, software ecosystems, turnarounds, and compliance.

What international detailing standards and codes do you adhere to?
+

Our detailers are fully certified and proficient in ACI 318-19, ACI 315 (Details and Detailing of Concrete Reinforcement), CRSI Manual of Standard Practice, BS 8666:2020, Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-1), IS 1786/IS 2502, and AS/NZS 4671. We configure drawing borders, bar mark naming conventions, and bend shapes strictly to your regional standard.

Which software applications do you utilize for 2D & 3D rebar detailing?
+

We operate an enterprise CAD/BIM environment including Tekla Structures (LOD 400), Autodesk Revit, RebarCAD, AutoCAD, and Navisworks Manage for clash analysis. We can deliver native RVT or Tekla models, open standard IFC files, and automated bender BVBS files for machine automation.

How do you handle structural RFIs (Requests For Information)?
+

During our initial Stage 02 Technical Review, any missing dimensions, unclear lap lengths, or joint rebar congestion issues are cataloged into a formal RFI log with proposed engineering solutions and sketch overlays. We ensure early resolution to prevent delays during detailing.

Can you produce automated Bar Bending Schedules for CNC shearing & bending machines?
+

Yes. Our BBS exports can be provided in standard formats including BVBS (format used by Schnell, MEP, Progress, and Pedax machines), as well as structured Excel, CSV, and PDF cut-lists.

What is your typical turnaround time for a structural rebar submittal package?
+

Turnaround depends on project tonnage and structural complexity. Standard foundation and initial column packages are typically delivered within 5 to 7 business days, with rapid 48-hour turnarounds available for urgent project milestones.
