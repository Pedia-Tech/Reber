// SYSTEMATIC EXECUTION THE 7-STEP REBAR PROTOCOL

From Design Drawings to Fabrication Floor

Quality is built into our sequence. Every project follows an audited 7-stage pipeline designed to detect omissions early, prevent site clashes, and deliver submittals on time.

01 STAGE: INTAKE

01 Drawings Received & Scope Indexing

We conduct a systematic intake of all design documentation, including structural design plans, architectural sections, geotechnical reports, general notes, specifications, and project pour schedules.

  • Drawing revision indexing and document register creation
  • Identification of governing codes (ACI, BS, Eurocode, regional annexes)
  • Verification of concrete clear cover schedules and grade specifications
  • Establishment of sheet borders, layers, and bar mark nomenclature conventions
PRIMARY INPUTS:
• Structural General Notes (SGN)
• Architectural Coordination Sets
• Regional Concrete Cover Criteria
• Concrete Pour Sequencing
STATUS: LOGGED & INDEXED GATE: INTAKE COMPLETE
02 STAGE: RFI AUDIT

02 Technical Review & RFI Resolution

A Senior Structural Engineer scrutinizes the structural design documents for missing dimensions, ambiguous lap lengths, conflicting MEP sleeves, or congested rebar nodes.

  • Formal logging of Requests For Information (RFIs) with proposed solutions
  • Detailed 2D/3D sketch markups illustrating the engineering conflict
  • Collaborative consultation with the structural Engineer of Record (EOR)
  • Resolution tracking to prevent detailing hold-ups or abortive work
ACTION PROTOCOL:
• Formal RFI Log Tracking
• Constructability Sketch Overlays
• Beam-Column Pass-Through Audit
• 48-Hour Rapid RFI Closure Goal
RFI LOG: SYSTEM TRACKED GATE: PRE-DETAILING CLEAR
03 STAGE: 3D MODELING

03 Rebar Detailing & 3D Modeling

Our certified detailers model every reinforcement element into placement drawings and BIM assemblies. Each bar is detailed with true bend radii, accurate hooks, and exact lap splice positions.

  • Production of detailed 2D placement drawings with clear bar mark callouts
  • Tekla Structures or Autodesk Revit LOD 400 3D solid rebar modeling
  • Staggered lap placement to avoid congestion and structural hot spots
  • Bar chairs, standoff supports, and rebar accessory coordination
CORE TOOLS:
• Tekla Structures LOD 400
• Autodesk Revit Rebar Engine
• RebarCAD Production Drafting
• Navisworks Clash Detection
MODELING: LOD 400 SOLID TOLERANCE: ±1.5MM
04 STAGE: BBS CUT-LISTS

04 BBS & Quantity Takeoff Extraction

Automated extraction of Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) directly from the detailed 2D/3D rebar elements. Every bar mark contains exact cut lengths, standard bending shapes, number of bars, and theoretical weight.

  • BS 8666, ACI 318, IS 2502, and AS/NZS compliant shape coding
  • Optimized cutting lengths to minimize scrap below 2% of total tonnage
  • Automated export to automated CNC rebar bending machinery (BVBS format)
  • Total steel tonnage summaries categorized by rebar diameter and grade
BBS OUTPUTS:
• BVBS Electronic Bender Files
• Excel & PDF Detailed Schedules
• Weight Summaries by Diameter
• Mill Stock Length Optimization
AUTOMATION: 100% LINKED SCRAP FACTOR: < 2%
05 STAGE: 3-TIER QA/QC

05 Three-Tier QA/QC Peer Audit

Independent peer verification protocol. A dedicated Senior Checker reviews every bar mark, dimension, lap splice, and clear cover against the structural design documents.

  • Level 1: Detailer Self-Audit against design documents
  • Level 2: Senior Technical Lead Review for joint congestion & constructability
  • Level 3: Independent QA Manager mathematical reconciliation of BBS vs drawings
  • Sign-off certification stamp applied to drawing package
CHECK CRITERIA:
• Cover & Lap Length Validation
• Mathematical Bar Quantity Match
• Bend Pin Radii Code Checks
• Zero Fabrication Clash Guarantee
AUDIT: 3-TIER PEER REVIEW GATE: CERTIFIED STAMP
06 STAGE: CLIENT APPROVAL

06 Client Review & Markup Incorporation

Submission of the drawing and BBS submittal package to the client, structural engineer, and general contractor for review and approval.

  • Complete PDF drawing set and digital BBS package submittal
  • Rapid incorporation of engineer markups and site revisions
  • Cloud-based markups and changelog revision tracking
  • Issuance of Revision clouds and detailed transmittal notices
COLLABORATION:
• Rapid 24-48hr Markup Turnaround
• Clear Revision Clouds & Deltas
• Submittal Tracking Log
• Engineer Approval as Noted (AAN)
REVISION: DELTA TRACKED APPROVAL: DOCUMENTED
07 STAGE: FABRICATION ISSUE

07 Final Delivery & Fabrication-Ready Issue

Approved drawings and schedules stamped "RELEASED FOR FABRICATION" (RFF). Files are transmitted directly to the steel fabricator's shearline and site placer ironworkers.

  • Fabrication-ready DWG, DXF, and vector PDF shop drawing packages
  • Electronic BVBS bender files directly fed into CNC automated bending machines
  • Detailed bar bending schedules in Excel and printable formats
  • Ongoing jobsite support and ironworker consultation through concrete pour
FINAL PACKAGE:
• DWG / DXF / Vector PDF
• BVBS Bender Machine Code
• Stamped Fabrication BBS
• 3D IFC Coordination Model
STATUS: RELEASED FOR FAB POUR SUPPORT: ACTIVE
