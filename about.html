The Three-Tier Verification Protocol

Every placement drawing and bar bending schedule is subject to our mandatory three-stage verification process prior to submission.

Level 01

Detailer Self-Audit

Primary author completes an itemized verification checklist before any file leaves their workstation:

  • Verify concrete clear covers against structural and durability specs
  • Confirm bar diameters and spacing against Engineer of Record (EOR) plans
  • Validate standard hook dimensions and pin diameters per standard code
Level 02

Technical Lead Review

A Senior Structural Rebar Specialist audits joint congestion, constructability, handling weights, and mechanical splice coupler layouts:

  • Beam-column joint congestion simulation & vibrator needle clearance
  • Cross-check with MEP penetrations, core drilling zones, and embeds
  • Optimization check for mill stock lengths to minimize scrap waste
Level 03

Final QA/QC Sign-Off

Independent Quality Assurance Manager completes a cross-referencing audit between the 2D placement drawings, 3D model, and BBS cut-lists:

  • 100% mathematical reconciliation of BBS bar quantities vs drawing marks
  • Sign-off stamped for direct fabrication & site release
  • Transmittal package prepared with revision changelogs and RFI history
