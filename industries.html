Engineering Detailing Across Critical Sectors

Different building typologies present unique constructability hurdles. Our specialized engineering squads adapt detailing methodologies, lap lengths, and coupler strategies to match your sector requirements.

SECTOR // 01

Commercial

Retail complexes, shopping malls, corporate headquarters, and mixed-use developments with demanding architectural layouts.

• Long-span post-tensioned slabs and transfer podium girders • Complex column re-alignments and offset column laps • Punching shear stud rails around heavily loaded column heads • Multi-tier basement car parks with earth-retaining diaphragm walls
SECTOR // 02

Residential

Multi-family high-density apartment blocks, modular residential pods, and luxury villa estates designed for fast floor-cycle construction.

• Highly standardized bar mark naming for repetitive typical floors • Prefabricated reinforcement roll-mats and welded wire mesh integration • Raft foundations on soil improvement or micro-piles • Acoustic and thermal break rebar connector details
SECTOR // 03

High-Rise

Towers exceeding 40 stories requiring high-strength concrete, heavily congested central core shear walls, and outrigger frames.

• Mechanical coupler stagger schedules (Type 1 and Type 2) • Diagonal reinforcement cages for link beams across elevator door bucks • Outrigger belt trusses with extreme high-tonnage rebar concentration • Concrete pour sequence and jump-form climbing clearance checks
SECTOR // 04

Industrial

Manufacturing facilities, data centers, oil & gas structures, turbine pads, and automated warehouse logistics centers.

• Dynamic load and vibration-resistant machine foundation mats • Heavy anchor bolt cage coordination and clash resolution • Heavy industrial floor slabs designed for heavy forklift wheel axle loads • Containment bund walls, drainage trenches, and sump pits
SECTOR // 05

Infrastructure

Bridges, elevated viaducts, transit tunnels, box culverts, retaining walls, highway interchanges, and water treatment tanks.

• Flared and tapered pier caps with 3D curved rebar geometry • Deep bored pile caps, diaphragm walls, and secant pile guide walls • Crack width control detailing per Eurocode 2 Part 2 / AASHTO • Epoxy-coated or stainless steel rebar schedules for marine environments
SECTOR // 06

Institutional

Hospitals, university campuses, research laboratories, airport terminals, and government security facilities.

• Heavy MEP service penetration trimmers and sleeve blockouts • Special moment-resisting frame (SMRF) seismic detailing • Radiation-shielded bunker walls for oncology and MRI medical suites • Blast-resistant perimeter structural reinforcement
