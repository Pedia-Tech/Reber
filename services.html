01 SVC: REBAR-DET

Rebar Detailing

Complete structural reinforcement detailing from foundation to roof slab. We translate design intent into precise engineering drawings indicating bar marks, grades, lap splices, hooks, and clearances.

  • Comprehensive plan, section, and isometric detail sheets
  • Staggered lap splice arrangements and seismic hook details
  • Clear cover checks tailored to environmental exposure classifications
  • Full compatibility with regional detailing manuals and project specifications
SHOP DRAWING SETS
OUTPUT: .DWG / .PDF SCALE: 1:20 / 1:50
02 SVC: BBS-AUTO

Bar Bending Schedules (BBS)

Automated, error-free bar bending schedules generated directly from placement drawings. Every bar mark contains exact cut lengths, standard bending shapes, number of bars, and theoretical weight in metric or imperial units.

  • Strict adherence to BS 8666, ACI 318, IS 2502, and AS/NZS shape coding
  • Optimized cutting lengths to minimize mill scrap below 2%
  • Automated export to automated CNC rebar bending machinery (BVBS format)
  • Separate bar lists by structural member and pour sequence
AUTOMATED CNC BENDING
FORMAT: BVBS / XLS / CSV TOLERANCE: CLASS A
03 SVC: PLACE-DWG

Placement Drawings

Site-oriented shop drawings created specifically for rebar placers and ironworkers. Features unambiguous labeling, color-coded callouts, chair layouts, and clear construction joint details.

  • Sequence of bar installation diagrams for congested joints
  • Bar chair and continuous bolster high chair layouts
  • Concrete pour joint stop-end details and starter dowel positions
  • Easy-to-read cross-sections corresponding directly to BBS bar marks
IRONWORKER PLACEMENT PLANS
IRONWORKER-READY POUR SEQUENCE TAGS
04 SVC: FOUND-DET

Foundation Rebar Detailing

Specialized reinforcement detailing for all substructure typologies: thick mat/raft foundations, pile caps, grade beams, strap footings, and basement elevator pits.

  • Multi-layer top and bottom rebar meshes with standoff chair schedules
  • Pile cap diagonal tension ties and anchorage hook verification
  • Elevator and pump pit step-down detailing with U-bar closures
  • Anchor bolt clearance coordination with structural steel columns
RAFT & PILE CAP MATS
RAFT / PILE / PAD WATERSTOP COORDINATED
05 SVC: COL-BEAM

Column & Beam Detailing

Rigorous superstructure detailing that guarantees site constructability. We model confined seismic tie zones, mechanical coupler staggers, beam pass-through steel, and shear links.

  • Seismic confinement hoops (135° hooks) per ACI 318 Chapter 18
  • Mechanical tension coupler layouts with stagger offsets
  • Deep transfer girders with heavy shear links and skin reinforcement
  • Beam-column joint congestion resolution prior to fabrication
MOMENT FRAME JOINTS
SPECIAL MOMENT FRAME SEISMIC DETAILING
06 SVC: SLAB-REIN

Slab Reinforcement

Detailing for two-way flat plates, beam-supported slabs, ribbed joist systems, and post-tensioned floors. Includes punching shear reinforcement, edge U-bars, and opening trimmers.

  • Top and bottom orthogonal reinforcement plans with lap zones clearly shown
  • Punching shear stud rails, shear bands, and column head stirrup cages
  • Trimmer bar reinforcement around mechanical and architectural duct openings
  • Detailed chair and bar support layouts to prevent mat deflection
SUSPENDED SLAB MATS
FLAT PLATE / POST-TENSION PUNCHING SHEAR RAILS
07 SVC: WALL-REIN

Wall Reinforcement

Detailing for concrete shear walls, elevator/stair cores, basement retaining earth walls, and retaining wing walls. Features boundary elements, cross-ties, and corner overlaps.

  • Boundary element confinement cages and cross-tie alternating schedules
  • Shear wall coupling beam detailing with diagonal rebar cages
  • Horizontal and vertical curtain rebar elevations
  • Earth retaining wall base key anchors and lap splice elevations
ELEVATOR & SHEAR CORES
SHEAR CORE / RETAINING BOUNDARY TIES CONFIRMED
08 SVC: QTO-ENG

Quantity Takeoff

Exacting rebar takeoffs for procurement and progress billing. Categorizes steel mass by rebar diameter, steel grade, structural level, and specific pour zone.

  • Breakdown of rebar tonnage by element: foundations, columns, beams, slabs, walls
  • Comprehensive accessory quantification: chairs, tie wire, couplers
  • Structured Excel reports compatible with ERP systems and billing spreadsheets
  • Continuous variance auditing as engineering design revisions occur
QUANTITY TAKEOFF AUDITING

09 SVC: REBAR-EST

Rebar Estimation

Pre-tender and budgetary rebar estimating based on conceptual or tender structural drawings. We calculate realistic reinforcement densities ($kg/m^3$ or $lbs/yd^3$) to protect bidding margins.

  • Precise reinforcement ratio benchmarks tailored by building typology
  • Scrap allowance factoring based on regional standard bar lengths
  • Labor installation difficulty ratings for complex structural geometries
  • Executive bid-summary sheets for fast-track tender submittals
INFRASTRUCTURE ESTIMATION
TENDER & PRE-BID DENSITY OPTIMIZED
10 SVC: BIM-3D

3D Rebar Modeling (BIM LOD 400)

Every physical rebar modeled in true 3D solid geometry using Tekla Structures and Autodesk Revit. Identifies physical collisions with post-tensioning anchorages, embedded structural plates, and MEP penetrations.

  • Level of Development (LOD) 400 certified rebar models
  • Multi-trade clash detection within Navisworks and BIM 360
  • Automated generation of 2D views directly linked to the 3D source
  • Sequenced 4D construction phasing models for site pour planning
